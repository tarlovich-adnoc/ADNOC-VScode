# Start here — the AI workflow in onehub-team

This repo comes ready for GitHub Copilot, set up by the ADNOC standard. The agents, prompts and rules
are already here — you don't install anything. **This page is the map.** Find the line that fits where
you are, and follow it.

## Brand new? Do these four, in order (about an hour)

1. **Connect Copilot to your tools** (one-time) — **[SETUP-COPILOT.md](SETUP-COPILOT.md)**. Do only the
   part your role needs: a developer or QA connects **Azure DevOps**; a BA or PM connects **Confluence**;
   a designer connects **Figma** (in your BY-ROLE section). The page tells you which is yours — skip the rest.
2. **Learn how you'll actually work** — the one-page daily guide, **[HOW_TO_USE.md](HOW_TO_USE.md)**.
3. **Find your role** — what you specifically can get done, and how — **[BY-ROLE.md](BY-ROLE.md)**.
4. **Try it hands-on** — five short missions for your first hour — **[ONBOARDING-QUEST.md](ONBOARDING-QUEST.md)**.

Any time something feels off, open Copilot Chat in **Agent mode** and type **`/copilot-doctor`** — it
checks the local repository setup in about 30 seconds, inventories configured MCP servers, and gives
you copy-ready prompts to verify each active connection.

## Not a developer?

You don't have to be. Whatever you do — business analysis, QA, project management, DevOps, design —
there's a section written for you in **[BY-ROLE.md](BY-ROLE.md)**. Do your setup, then read your role.
